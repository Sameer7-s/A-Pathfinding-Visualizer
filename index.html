QUICK GUIDE

Make a path your own.

  1. Place obstaclesSelect Wall and drag on the grid. Use Eraser to remove walls.
  2. Choose your endpointsSelect Start or Goal, then click a cell.
  3. Run the searchUse Find shortest path, or Step to advance one event at a time.
  4. Compare algorithmsCompare A*, Dijkstra, and BFS on the same grid.

For a fair comparison, BFS assumes every move has equal cost. With diagonal movement enabled, A* and Dijkstra use 1 and √2 movement costs, while BFS still minimizes number of moves, not geometric distance.
